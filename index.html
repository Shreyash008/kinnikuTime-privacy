Kinniku Time — Privacy Policy

Last updated 24 July 2026

Kinniku Time keeps your fitness data yours. This policy summarises what the app reads, stores, and shares, and the controls you have. It is written to match how the app actually works.

What we read from Health Connect

Only if you connect Health Connect and grant permission, the app reads — read-only — your steps, body weight, and active/total calories burned. This is used to show your activity on Home and to refine your calorie and weight targets. We never write data back to Health Connect. You can review or revoke this access at any time from the Health Connect settings on your device.

What we store and sync

When you create an account, your email and the data you log — workouts, nutrition, habits, weigh-ins, plans, and preferences — are stored in your account so they sync across your devices. You can sign in with email and password or with Google; signing in with Google uses Google's sign-in to verify you and shares basic account info (your email) to set up your account. Sync is provided by Supabase. Your data is scoped to your account and protected by row-level security, so other users can't read it. Your sign-in session is stored encrypted on the device using the Android Keystore.

Microphone & voice logging

If you use voice logging to dictate a set, the app records audio only while voice input is active and sends it to your device's speech recognition service (typically Google's) to convert it to text. The app doesn't store the recording — it keeps only the text you confirm.

Third-party services we contact

  • Food search & barcodes — USDA FoodData Central and Open Food Facts. Barcode scanning runs in Google Play Services on your device.
  • Exercise catalog & form demos — wger and ExerciseDB.
  • Accounts & sign-in — Supabase, and Google if you use Google sign-in.
  • Voice logging — your device's speech recognition service (typically Google's).

We send only what a request needs (for example a search term or a barcode). The app does not sell your data.

Data kept on your device

The app keeps a local copy of your data so it works offline. Signing out clears that local copy from the device; it is restored from your account when you sign back in.

Your controls

  • Disconnect Health Connect any time from your device settings.
  • Sign out to remove the local copy of your data from a device.
  • To request deletion or a copy of your account data, contact us below.
